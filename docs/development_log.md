## Project Overview

MoneyMnetor is a website dedicated to educate teenagers and upcoming college students on the topic of financial education. This website was orginated from the observation that simply many young adults have no idea of finance tips and tricks until it s too late and they will have to make important decisions with little to no knowlege of the consequences.

The project evolved into a React-based financial education application with AI-assisted explanations, financial discovery cards, and interactive financial scenarios.

 ## Challenges Faced
 Some challenges that let me learn more about my project was the implementation of the AI, UI development after the initial examples given by freebuff, creating features that were not just adding a chatbot, connecting sources to the AI so information can be pulled reliably.

## Development Challenges and What I Learned

### 1. AI Implementation
One of my biggest challenges was connecting Groq AI to MoneyMentor and making sure it worked properly. I encountered problems with API connections, backend errors, and getting the AI to respond correctly. Fixing these issues helped me understand how the frontend, backend, and AI communicate.

### 2. UI Development
Freebuff provided the initial UI examples, but I wanted to expand the design and make it fit my vision for MoneyMentor. I worked on modifying components, improving the layout, and connecting new features to the interface. This helped me learn more about React, JavaScript, and how interactive websites function.

### 3. Creating Unique Features
I wanted MoneyMentor to offer more than a basic AI chatbot. I developed features such as Discovery Cards and Spot the Catch to help teenagers discover financial opportunities and practice making financial decisions. This taught me how to create features that make an application more useful and engaging.

### 4. Connecting Reliable Sources
Another challenge was making sure the AI could provide trustworthy financial information instead of relying entirely on its own knowledge. I worked on connecting financial evidence from official sources such as the IRS, CFPB, and Federal Student Aid to the AI. This helped me understand the importance of accurate information and the challenges of making AI responses reliable.
