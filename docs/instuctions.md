## How to Use MoneyMentor

 - Discover Financial Opportunities: Explore AI-generated cards about college, credit, banking, investing, and employment.

 - Explore: Click a discovery card to read its explanation, suggested actions, and available official references.

- Discover More: Generate additional financial learning opportunities.

- Ask MoneyMentor AI: Enter financial questions and receive educational explanations.

- Spot the Catch: Click Start Scenario, choose an answer, and review the explanation and reference.

## Important Notes

MoneyMentor is an educational application. AI-generated information may contain inaccuracies, and official references do not guarantee that every statement has been independently verified.
